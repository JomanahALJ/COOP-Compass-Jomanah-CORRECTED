About COOP Compass

COOP Compass is a website designed to help university students explore cooperative training opportunities and prepare for their future careers.

Our Goal

We aim to make training preparation easier by providing useful information in a simple and organized place.

What we help students do

  • Explore different career paths.
  • Understand the skills needed for each field.
  • Organize their training preparation.
